# What we learned the hard way

From our Bank of America Affordable Housing Challenge 2026 project: 61 homes in Altadena for Eaton Fire families.

## What went wrong

- The pro forma took weeks of hand-built spreadsheets
- Every rate change meant rebuilding the numbers
- Reviewers caught mismatched totals, money in the wrong phase, missing contingency
- One mistake can cost a whole funding round

## What every housing team needs

- [ ] **Does it pencil?** A clear yes or no
- [ ] **Who closes the gap?** Ranked, with apply links
- [ ] **The best move**, not a spreadsheet
- [ ] **Tell me when anything changes**
- [ ] **No pro forma by hand**
- [ ] **A second reader before reviewers**
- [ ] **For donors and families too**
