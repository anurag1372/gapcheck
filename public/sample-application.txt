HARBORLIGHT COMMONS — FUNDING APPLICATION (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE FOR DEMO)
Applicant: Harborlight Homes (demo nonprofit)
Site: 2400 N. Lake Ave, Altadena, CA (wildfire rebuild parcel)

1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
Harborlight Commons is a 61-unit new-construction affordable rental community on a parcel cleared after the Eaton fire. The existing damaged structure will be demolished. Total development cost is $38,400,000. The project's must-pay debt has a debt service coverage ratio of 1.20x. Construction start is planned 4 months after award.

2. PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The building is five stories with 58 units (12 studios, 26 one-bedroom, 20 two-bedroom) and 44 parking spaces.
